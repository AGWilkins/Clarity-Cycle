About Andrew Wilkins

More than thirty years in high-stakes decision-making taught Andrew that better thinking is a trainable skill, not a personality trait.

Andrew has spent more than three decades working in financial markets as a trader, portfolio manager, risk executive and regulatory consultant.

His career has ranged from open-outcry trading pits and hedge fund portfolio management to electronic trading, market surveillance, algorithmic controls and board-level risk oversight. He has held senior roles across trading, compliance, risk and technology, advising both global institutions and fast-growing firms.

Alongside his professional work, Andrew has spent a lifetime studying decision-making, attention, performance and human behaviour. A martial artist for more than fifty years, he has long been fascinated by the relationship between perception, judgement and action.

He is also a writer, speaker and practitioner of artificial intelligence, with a particular interest in how technology is changing the way people think, learn and make decisions.

Andrew is the creator of Clarity Cycle, a practical system designed to improve how people pay attention, recognise patterns, make decisions and think more clearly through short daily exercises.

He lives in Essex with his beautiful wife Sadie and continues to write, build, teach and ask awkward questions.

Why Clarity Cycle exists

Most of us do not need more information. We need better attention.

Clarity Cycle exists to train the cognitive skills beneath almost everything else: attention, pattern recognition, decision-making and the ability to notice automatic behaviour before it takes over.
